## Coffee, shall we?

Hello! I'm Kim, a Senior Software Engineer based in Okinawa, Japan 🇯🇵 

I made a career switch into software engineering during the pandemic, and five years in, I'm still hooked. I've spent most of that time building fintech products end-to-end across ASEAN markets, and I'm currently building an AI agent platform at a pre-launch startup.

What I bring beyond the code is the combination of both worlds: fluency with AI-assisted development, and a product mindset that keeps the end user in focus.

Fueled by caffeine, and life on the island.
